## The question

How should the support master minimise split units per channel exactly: which formulation lane B2
(#103) builds, what B1 (#99) and B4 (#104) become, how a minimum is certified, and what any of it
means once every district must be ZIP-connected on the drawn polygons (M1).

## Situation

All four seats read the split arithmetic as formulable and small (at most a few dozen candidate
units per channel), and the missing object as liftability: which master plans have an M1 drawing.
Sol and Astra called it a frame problem (optimise plans, certify drawings separately); Opus and
Fable a survey among lanes with a framing defect under M1 ("split" and "connected" defined on
different objects). Every SPLITS.md number is unit-level (SPLITS §1 note).

## Owner decisions taken during the council (2026-10-05)

Asked after pass 1 (all four seats raised them), recorded on #91:

1. **What a split is under M1.** A district that owns any ZCTA of a state, zero-opportunity ones
   included, has split that state. The count is taken on the drawn map (polygon ownership).
2. **Cuts break ties.** Rank: split units, then cuts (Σ over split states of districts − 1), then
   visual defects, shape, balance. CA in 4 beats CA in 5 at equal split count.
3. **Candidate set.** The certified minimum ranges over all states, not a free list; the states it
   names are reported, and any ban is explicit with its cost.
4. **Report form.** s* is a certified unit-level floor; each drawn M1 map is reported as s* + g;
   "minimal" only when g = 0.

### The 13-split design (C4, conditional, not run)

Forced-only infeasibility gives s ≥ |Φ| + 1 by up-closure under μ = 0. If #94's 1.15τ forced sets
equal the 1.1τ sets the sweep used (WH {CA, FL}, FI {CA, FL, NY, OH, PA}) and the forced-only master
at exactly δ = 0.15 is proved infeasible for WH and FI, then WH ≥ 3 and FI ≥ 6, national = 4 if all
of CA FL NY TX exceed 1.15τ at K 15, and 13 is the unit-level minimum for the NE + plains design.
The shrink caveat is real (lane B: Φ(1.10τ) = {v1} infeasible yet s* = 1 by another unit). The
sweep record (scenario-sweeps line 38) gives no margin, gap or status. Three fixed-δ solves would
settle it. Caveats: that design fails the WH $ rule (WH 12 at $845M), the 13-split map fails M1, and
by finding 14 an η > 0 floor is not a floor on M1 maps.

## Recommendation (converged)

- **B2 (#103):** F2 with the aggregated big-M row, the exact-flag row, fixings (s_v = 1 when
  M_v > U_c; no s_v for units forced whole), candidate set all states. Passes: (1) min Σ s, pin
  Σ s ≤ s*; (2) min cuts, pin (decision 2); (3) min diameter; (4) balance LPs with the split set
  fixed, worst deviation then spread. Hall rows: optional strengthening (Opus: include; Sol, Astra:
  optional). Pin the chosen set, not only Σ s, in later passes (Fable).
- **B1 (#99):** a cuts instrument, never a splits proxy (λ|S| counts Σ (r_v − 1)); it becomes pass 2's
  pin. Avoid the blended large λ.
- **B4 (#104):** a policy restriction, run as a variant reporting Δs* per ρ; never silently hard.
- **Certification:** finding 4, with the floor labelled by its policy class. Under M1 the floor must
  come from an η-free bounding master (finding 15) or be labelled "over 𝒳_c(δ) only".
- **Under M1:** certify the floor, measure g with #109's realizer, and close g only with a lift and
  no-good cuts that obey finding 10 (the three-level method: choose T, plan (n,t), lift each v ∈ T).
- **Before B2 code (Fable):** the three fixed-δ solves of C4, cheap and a real-data test of the F6
  logic.

## Confidence and what would change it

14–17 as refutations; the fix in 15 is a bound checked on ≤ 3,744 small instances and is not built.

drawn, would change the recommendation.
