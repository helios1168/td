# P4: Open-source districting tools, and fixed target vs fixed K
Two parts. (a) Tools: for GerryChain (Python, MGGG), GerryChainJulia, the exact districting codes
of Validi-Buchanan-Lykhovyd (github: "districting-models"), Gurnee-Shmoys ("fairmandering"),
Swamy et al. ("optimal districting"), and the hypergraph partitioners KaHyPar / Mt-KaHyPar,
hMETIS, PaToH: from the primary documentation or paper, record what each takes as input (graph
format, weights), which constraints it can enforce or score (contiguity and how, population
balance window, county/unit split counting, a per-district limit on split units, compactness
measures, any "no thin connection" check), its licence, language, and scale reported. Mark
"claimed" anything not read in the primary docs. (b) Fixed target vs fixed K: in districting
and territory design, what is said about balance defined as a fixed window per district with a
free number of districts, versus a fixed K with a band around total/K? Find papers that treat K
as a decision variable (variable number of territories, "p" free, "districting with an unknown
number of districts", bin-packing-type territory design) and what they report about solve
difficulty and bounds compared with fixed K. Deliver a table for (a) and a short answer with
sources for (b).
