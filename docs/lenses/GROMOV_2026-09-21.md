## Moves that fired

### 4. Step down to a surrogate, then audit the step *(attested attitude, constructed audit)*

The chain from the honest problem to what runs: joint (map, roster, channel plan) under Nash → two-stage draw-then-match (a business constraint, explicitly **not** derived — incumbency 0.89 nats against balance 0.148) → per-bundle separable draw → support master over states and macro units → transportation LP per split unit → argmax rounding + ≤5 Lloyd rounds → contiguous recut → fragment healing with **no capacity guard** → national residual routing that adds unplanned mass → a cell ledger resolved by bundle cardinality → placeholder rep IDs.

**Audit 1 — is the surrogate easy?** The master is, and that is the finding: §3.2's objective is `min 0.001 Σ_S Diam(S)·n_S` at `mip_rel_gap` 0.01–0.05. It optimizes essentially nothing; its content is the *feasible set*, i.e. the support family — which §3.1 says is enumerated under caps, then filtered to remove combinations, then hand-appended (the seven-unit New England-plus-NY support), with distance overrides past 1,200 km. **The district footprints are largely authored; the solver picks multiplicities and shares.** Downstream is where the difficulty went, and it is not easy: six heuristic stages whose stated guard is "do not worsen excess", not "make feasible". The retreat bought an easy master at the price of an uncertified realizer. The surrogate got easy. The problem didn't.

**Audit 2 — price the retreat.** Solve the master to zero gap on a validated support family. What fraction of the owner's question is answered?

- *How many districts per channel* — partly, and from outside the model: K and the band are inputs, and 36 count vectors were enumerated by hand with no criterion to rank them (§4.3 calls the recommended baseline "a historical selection").
- *Who staffs each territory* — **nothing.** Placeholder `R0001`; 235 district–scenario combinations carry conflicting rep labels.
- *Minimize friction* — **nothing.** No objective at any level reads a book. `MODEL_FULL` §6 says so deliberately: a rep term in level 0 is bilinear and was refused. Books enter exactly once, at the last step, on a frozen map — and in the shipped catalog that step was never run.

So the rigor budget is spent almost entirely in the layer whose output feeds the layer that answers nothing.

**Re-run of the audit across time — this is the Stopping scaffold's second test, and it fails.** `FRAME` §3's acceptance items 4 (per-wholesaler continuity), 5 (a bound in business units) and the hand-drawn baseline have been open since 2026-09-02. Between then and today the artifact grew from one k=18 map to 36 scenarios and 6,082,380 rows. Nothing in that growth touches the three unmet criteria. The third test fails too: formulations are getting longer, not shorter. Only question yield still passes, which is why the verdict is "change the object", not "stop".

**The tell.** `run_vary_wifi_grid.py` writes the literal `100% Contiguous (0 violations)` into `summary.csv` even when its own check failed. The deliverable's only quality column is a constant. That is what a surrogate looks like after it has quietly become the problem: the reported property describes the pipeline, not the map.

### 9. If you don't understand, count *(attested)*

Three counts nobody has done. (Move 1 rides on (c): the within-scenario spread *is* the "is the fine structure visible from far away?" test.)

**(a) The headcount arithmetic contradicts itself.** $48B ÷ $1B ⇒ 46–53 districts. The file carries 113 reps (v3 CONUS) / 131 (v4). One rep per district, one district per rep ⇒ 60–85 unmatched, 53–65% of the pool. The escape that made the single-channel 6.3:1 ratio sane — `PROBLEM.md` §3, "the unmatched keep covering other manufacturers in the existing FI/wirehouse channel" — **does not survive CWIFI**, because the model now covers all of N+WH+FI+other. There is no other channel left to absorb them. So one of four things is false and no document says which: the $1B target, the go-forward roster, one-rep-per-district (`FRAME` A13), or reps-carry-a-channel-tag (★C, open). Each changes K, which is the first thing the owner asked about.

**(b) The friction ceiling — the bound this project computes on the other axis and has never computed on this one.** `PROBLEM.md` §7 insists the balance ceiling be computed before any solver runs; it is, it is free, it needs no confidential data, and the delivered draw sits 8.2e-5 nats under it. The analogue: relax geometry entirely, let the K districts be arbitrary ZIP sets, maximize retained book over injective rep→district maps. An upper bound on any map's retention, from books and K alone, no solver. The single-channel end of the ladder was measured (84.17% of book at k=18); the multi-channel version does not exist. Without it, "this map retains X%" has no denominator. It may rank or kill count vectors before a single map is drawn.

**(c) Signal against noise in the catalog.** Two spreads, same units:
- *across-scenario*: retention under real-book matching, 36 scenarios;
- *within-scenario*: retention under n re-realizations of one scenario (reseed Lloyd, permute bundle claim order, toggle `--split-cut`, toggle `--band-slack`).

If within ≥ across, the catalog cannot be chosen on friction, the ZIP layer sits below the decision's resolution, and the count decision belongs to operations with any feasible realizer. If across ≫ within, friction is the missing tiebreaker and the ranking is immediate. Either verdict retires a large amount of current work, and neither number exists. Note that the lever is already built: `channel.score_draws` keeps the best-staffing draw from a portfolio, stage 2 is milliseconds, and it is not wired into the grid. The experiment that measures the noise is the same machinery that converts it to signal.

### 5 (with 7). Build the language in which the question becomes askable; purge the words that think for you *(attested; the lens treats 5/6/7 as one move seen from three sides)*

The owner's criterion is "keep existing wholesalers covering their current territories as much as possible." There is no friction metric in the repository. No retention column in `summary.csv`. `share_of_book_kept` is specified once in `agy-job/contract.md` and computed for nothing. `FRAME` §3.4: "never produced."

What is missing is not a number. It is the object the number would be about:

- The **pre-merger state is a cover, not a partition.** A rep's territory is *defined* (`decisions/app-2026-09-08`) as exactly the ZIPs where they have sales. Two firms both hold book in 671 ZIPs carrying 48.8% of mapped opportunity; the three-channel export's own report counts 23,900 of 33,214 (zip, channel) cells with 2+ candidate reps, max 5.
- The **post-merger state is a partition** into ~50 cells, one rep each.
- **Friction is a distance from a cover to a partition, and no such distance is defined here.** Until one is, "minimize friction" cannot be evaluated, optimized, or reported, and the 36 scenarios cannot be ranked. Three candidate coordinates, all computable on the work machine from books plus a map, none needing a solver: per-rep share of own book inside the matched district; reps unmatched and their book; book mass changing hands, split by whether it crosses the legacy firm boundary.

**Four words doing unearned work:**

- **"master"** — a feasibility oracle with a 0.001·diameter tie-break over a hand-curated support family. The word implies the plan is derived where it is substantially authored. Call it the *support feasibility pass*.
- **"contiguous"** — three different objects wear it: connected supports on the state graph, `--split-cut contiguous` (a greedy grower), and a constant string in `summary.csv`. None of them is ZIP-graph contiguity of the delivered map, which §3.5 says must be checked separately and is not.
- **"scenario"** — today a 4-integer count vector. The owner's scenario is a count vector *plus a roster plus a friction number*. The word promises the second and ships the first.
- **"greenfield"** — settled 2026-08-31 with the justification "the carve-out has no bilateral overlap structure; the pair census does not apply." That reason is specific to the **national** carve-out. It has been inherited by WH, FI and WIFI — existing channels, with incumbent coverage, and on the export's own count heavy bilateral overlap. **The word carried an assumption across a boundary its own reason does not cross.** That is the frame finding, and it is why the situation resolves from audit to frame.

## What the problem lacks

- **A friction metric in business units, computed for any delivered map.** Everything above waits on it.
- **Real rep IDs on the catalog.** The staffing half of the deliverable has never been run on the object being shipped.
- **A friction ceiling.** Without it, retention numbers have no denominator.
- **A baseline.** Friction is a difference from the status quo; a difference needs two maps and only one exists. `FRAME`'s hand-drawn state-grouped alignment is open since 2026-09-02 and is cheap.
- **A declared status-quo map.** "Territory = support of the book" is an inference the business has never confirmed.
- **Certainty that WH/FI/WIFI carry rep books at all in the live file.** If books are national-only, friction is definable on 37.6% of the mass and undefined on the rest.
- **Provenance tags on output.** §3.6 shows solver-certified, independently-measured, and asserted values sharing one table with no way to tell them apart.
