The graph has 11,695 rook edges and spans 49 states, and every state's induced subgraph is connected. `geo.zip_graph` on it takes about 3.2 s on m5. These hold for seed 0 and 4,000 draws only; another seed or size can lose a cell.
- **Population concentration.** Among the 4,000 ZCTAs drawn at seed 0, their own `pop2025` has Gini 0.545, top-10% share 0.41 and top-1% share 0.115. Over all 33,300 CONUS ZCTAs, population has Gini 0.68, top-10% share 0.517 and top-1% share 0.154 (the last two from the curator's rerun). Drawing by population flattens the concentration, so population-weighted masses fall below the fixture's Gini and top-10% bands. That is why the masses are lognormal and independent of population.
