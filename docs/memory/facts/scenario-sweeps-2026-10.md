## Channel shape
- National and FI are fine-grained (heaviest ZIP 0.13τ and 0.10τ; 7 and 14 ZIPs above 5%τ; 56% and 48% of opportunity in ZIPs below 1%τ); WH is lumpier (0.22τ, 31 ZIPs, 21%). Merging channels at the same total K makes ZIPs lumpier relative to τ (WH+FI 0.25τ, national+WH 0.20τ, all five 0.28τ, IFA 0.36τ), because channels peak in the same ZIPs.
- National is concentrated: CA, TX, NY, FL and NJ hold $10.1B of $17.5B.
- Planning channels solve independently once the WIFI region and national routing are fixed, so a scenario grid factorizes into one table per channel and combinations are scored afterwards without new solves.

## What decides balance
- **The 900 km distance cap is what makes WIFI necessary.** With the mountain/plains states in national, WH or FI, the smallest band is 60–80%; at 1,600 km with no WIFI, national 13 / WH 12 / FI 23 draws every district within ±5%.
- Council 2026-10-01 (four seats, graded T, proof unchecked): WH's 1.615-district western island sets a −19.25% floor for every connected WH map at K 11 under the 51's domains; the fixed WIFI blocks cap the worst deviation near 88%; the margin-free master is the lower bound and an audited drawing the upper.
- Each separate part of a channel takes a whole number of districts (owner's feasibility conditions, 2026-10-01), so balance depends on K values that suit each part, not dollar targets: national 13 or 16 and WH 12 balance well, national 14 and WH 11 don't; IFA draws best at K 49 (worst −9.6%).
- **Looks sweep** (margin off, mountain/plains at 1,600 km): national 16, WH 12, FI 24 draws 52 of 52 within ±10% with 18 split states (the "18-split" map, `A_fi1600_v1_na16_WH12_FI24`, reproducible only with the margin off and ZIP 13027's FI cell zeroed, `run3.py` on m5). 8 of its 18 splits come from states that could stay whole. Keeping all districts within ±10% took about 18 splits; at 14–15 splits, 44–47 of 52 met it.
- **Fewer splits:** with forced splits only, WH 12 (CA only) is infeasible even at ±20%, and FI 24 needs ±12% (smallest band 11.44% whatever third state is added; TN, 1.07 districts, likely binds); FI 24 plans inside ±10% (9.85%) only with TN and NC added at η 0.15. Forced-only plans draw wider than planned (national 16 at ±10% draws up to 11.2%; planning at ±8% drew 12.2%), because plans sit at the band edge and whole-ZIP drawing adds 1–3 points. Contact caps don't bind in the 18-split map, and tight caps make FI infeasible below 27.8%; the visible mess comes from thin links (all of them New England reaches from NY or NJ).
- **Combined New England channel:** New England holds $2.30B across national, WH and FI and splits whole-state into CT+RI ($1.07B) and MA+NH+VT+ME ($1.23B). With it at K 2 plus national 15, WH 12, FI 22: 51 of 51 within ±10% (worst 9.6%) with 16 splits. NE plus the nine-state okks plains block with national 15, WH 12, FI 20: 50 of 50 within ±10% (worst 9.9%) with 14 splits, the fewest so far, and passes the audit (superseded the same day by the 13-split map below). A middle block cuts WH into west and east parts, so WH's K must suit the western part; WH 10 can't plan within ±10% with a combined channel. The combined channel's states-per-district cap must cover its largest whole block.
- The sweep runner `run3` (m5, gitignored `runs/`) exits 0 to the dispatcher even when the td run fails: count drawn runs by `"rc": 0` in each `.out` or by `districts.csv`, never by folders.
- With the may-split list cut to states over 0.75 of a district, national (CA FL NJ NY TX) still plans at 7–9% but WH's smallest band rises to about 29%; WH's mid-size Northeast states (PA, NJ, NY at 0.74–0.82 of a district) must split for WH to balance. In option A (13/12/23, no WIFI, 1,600 km) FI split 14 states, and 28 of national's 60 district pieces were under 10% of their district; η limits pieces relative to the state, not the district. At ±10% the forced splits (states over 1.1 districts) are 10 for 16/12/24 and 11 with New England combined. A picker that ranked "at most 4 states per district" ahead of balance chose national at ±30% over ±10%: rank balance first.

## NE + okks follow-up: 13 splits, FI's east, the Philadelphia metro (2026-10-02)

Same session (01a0f177, m5), later on 2026-10-02. Scenario: the combined New England plus okks plains channel with national 15, WH 12, FI 20.
- **The combined channel was the most effective looks lever:** New England (CT+RI, MA+ME+NH+VT) plus the nine okks plains states took splits from 18 to 13 at 50 of 50 within ±10%. The remaining defects are FI shapes in the Mid-Atlantic that K, caps and a single metro carve can't fix; they need td#80, #81 or #59.
- **Forced splits only** (states above 1.1 districts) leave WH unable to plan below ±19.6% (CA, FL only) and FI below ±17.5% (CA FL NY OH PA). WH needs PA (0.90 of a district) and FI needs NC. National is fine without NJ: CA, FL, NY and TX alone give 15 of 15 within ±10%, worst 9.7%, with a 12 km NY link and a NY piece at 19% of its district.
- **The 13-split map:** with national split only in CA, FL, NY and TX (and the combined channel at 3), 50 of 50 districts draw within ±10% and the audit passes, with 13 split states: N CA FL NY TX, WH CA FL PA, FI CA FL NC NY OH PA. That is the forced 11 plus WH PA and FI NC, the minimum found for this design. National keeps NJ whole, at the cost of N_13 taking a 19% NY piece through a 12 km link.
- **FI's eastern mess in that map** comes from PA (1.65 districts) and OH (1.16) each being cut three ways, NJ (0.71) borrowing pieces of PA and NY, and NC (0.83) split between FI_13 and FI_08. That gives a stretched FI_17 (VA+OH+WV) and FI_08 (GA+FL+NC), although every district is within ±7%.
- **Best eastern FI shape:** among 112 FI plans within ±8.5% (FI K 19–22, caps on and off), the best at K 20 is the 13-split map's FI. FI 21 and 22 can't plan below ±8.4% and ±13.5%. FI 19 (±7.3%, 4 states per district) draws 19 of 19 within ±10% (worst 9.6%) with no FI thin links or small pieces and cuts OH in 2 instead of 3, but adds TN and TX splits and a stretched FI_01 (north TX + AR + MS + AL). PA stays in 3 pieces at both K, and capping FI districts per state at 2 didn't give a better eastern shape.
- **Carving the Philadelphia metro** (CBSA 37980) as a unit gives FI a whole-metro district and a VA+MD+DC district, and removes VA+OH+WV. But PA outside the metro (1.24 FI districts) is still cut three ways, FI_07 takes southern DE across the bay, and WH re-cut to 11 of 12 within ±10% (worst 14%). Metros are global units in `td/spec.py`, so one carve replans national and WH too. Counted by unit there are 15 split states against 13 without the metro, and the looks scorer counts a whole-metro district as splitting PA, NJ, DE and MD.
