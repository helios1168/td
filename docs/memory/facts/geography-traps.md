# Geography traps (AGENTS.md traps 21, 22, 23)

The evidence behind `AGENTS.md` traps 21, 22 and 23. Filed 2026-09-28 from td#54 ([comment](https://github.com/helios1168/td/issues/54#issuecomment-5866766796), m2 session 01a0e728 fork 01a0e738). The pre-#54 wording is `git show b7a74c9:AGENTS.md` traps 21 to 23. Every number is from 2026-09-09, measured on the pre-refactor pipeline (tag `archive/pre-support-2026-09`) with its `geom.json` and CONUS instance. The full accounts are `mem:geo/zip-adjacency` and `mem:geo/zcta-geometry`.

- **Trap 21, the vertex set.** The contiguity graph's vertex set shipped separately, as `proximity_zips` in `geom.json`. A zip whose Voronoi cell clips away on the coastline has a ZCTA but no cell. Inferring vertices from the drawn cells therefore admits isolated vertices, and that made the split's contiguity guard infeasible.
- **Trap 22, vintage.** Between the 2020 and 2025 gazetteers, 468 zips move more than 1 km and 70 more than 5 km. The 2020 file has no point for 9 of the 3,713 live zips. Switching vintage alone moved 31 zips on the committed k = 18 map. The new pipeline takes every geography input at 2025 vintage (`mem:decisions/support-refactor-2026-09-25`), so the old 2020 pin does not carry over.
- **Trap 23, two tessellations.** Over the 3,704 placed zips, ZCTA polygon adjacency and the Voronoi rook graph share 4,462 edges, out of 10,483 and 4,843 respectively (Jaccard 0.411). Real ZCTA adjacency alone gives 816 components, 474 of them singletons. That is why the Voronoi graph was the contiguity model. On 2026-09-28 the #54 handoff marked trap 23 provisional until #57 ("Decide the authoritative ZIP graph") settles which graph the new pipeline uses.
  - Correction, 2026-09-28 (td#57, the owner's OD2 answer in m5 session 01a0e73e): trap 23 is no longer provisional. The authoritative graph is the Voronoi rook graph of 2025 gazetteer points over the extract's placed ZIPs, with an explicit vertex list (`decisions/open-decisions-2026-09-28`). The 2026-09-09 numbers above come from the earlier vintage and are not current measurements.
