# The support pipeline before the refactor, 2026-09-25

Measured on m5 in sessions 01a0d8f6 and 01a0d98f, 2026-09-25, on `main` before td#52. What the owner decided to do about it: `mem:decisions/support-refactor-2026-09-25`.

- **Import closure.** The support pipeline's import closure is 38 files: 7 core-algorithm files (5,041 lines), 16 legacy files pulled in for about 8 helpers from `full_plan` and `level0` (8,549), 9 near-duplicate drivers (5,932) and 6 output files (4,179). It reaches `level0`, `milp_engines`, `atoms`, private helpers of `full_plan`, and `stage2_state`: about 23k of the 56k lines in `td/` and `tools/`. The legacy code can be deleted only after those helpers are extracted. The master MILP itself is about 100 lines in `run_wifi_grid.solve_generic_channel`.
- **The 36-scenario catalog can't be reproduced.** Neither Studio holds the confidential instance or any `battery/results` output (checked on m5 and m2), and the scenario drivers read earlier solves through hard-coded paths into a torn-down sandbox.
- **Ownership drift is upstream of the export.** The export already enforces whole-cell ownership: one district per (ZIP, source channel), with no share column. Upstream, the realizer silently rescales state targets (`td/solvers/state_splits.py:1000`), turns shares under 100% into an `other` pseudo-district (`tools/plan_realise.py:249`), and solver reports compute masses from planned shares (`tools/solve_global_multichannel.py:335`).
- **Rounding.** `td/solvers/centers.py:244` rounds the transportation LP with `X.argmax(axis=1)`, which bounds nothing about a district's mass error (`kb/assignment-lp-rounding.md` in sv-ntlee).
- **Exporter channels.** `export_instance.py` accepts only the channel names in `CHANNEL_ALIASES` and rejects any other, so a fresh extract with new channels needs exporter v3 first.
- **Support family closure (claim, to be verified in B2).** The family built by `generate_valid_supports` (connected, size cap, pairwise distance) is closed under connected subsets, so D1 option 2 comes down to "a support with a whole unit has multiplicity ≤ 1". National's appended and hand-filtered supports break that closure.
- **Plan draft.** Revisions 1 to 6 were drafted in `~/drafts/td-support-refactor-plan.md` on m5, which no sync copies; revision 6 is filed verbatim as td#52. The m5 draft was kept while the plan is under review; the issue body becomes the copy of record once the §7 issues exist.
