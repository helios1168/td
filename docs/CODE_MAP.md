## What a worktree must hand-copy

Nothing. The confidential extract, `instance_descaled*.json.gz`, stays at the hub root and is
gitignored, and so do the public 2025 files in `data/public/` (`tl_2025_us_state.zip`,
`tl_2025_us_zcta520.zip`); a worktree reads them from `$TD_REPO`.  `tools/maps/render.py` reads
its untracked inputs from `$TD_REPO/runs/` on m5 (the legacy ZCTA shapefile and geo cache under
`runs/sweep/grid_2026-10-01/present/legacy/archive/`, the ZIP pages' cache
`runs/autonomous_2026-10-05/batch/geo/`, the `$` factors `runs/sweep/grid_2026-10-01/tables.json`),
or from its flags.
