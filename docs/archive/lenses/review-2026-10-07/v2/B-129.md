## Goal
For a split state and the pieces planned for it, assign every ZCTA of the state to a piece so
that each piece, joined to its attach states, passes the exact M1 gate inside its mass window. The
carve returns `ok` (checked by the gate), `infeasible` (proved), or `unknown`. This is part B of
the fewest-splits pipeline (owner, 2026-10-07), built against contract v2 alongside #103 and
#130.

## Plan
1. **Order of attempts per job** (`tools/exp/contig/carve.py`):
   1. **Constructive search.**
      - Seeds: each piece starts at its attach bodies, or at its heaviest ZCTA when the attach set
        is empty.
      - Optional seeds come from the state's split in the nearest existing drawn ledger, or from
        #127's county pieces. They are seeds only, never fixed boundaries (OD4).
      - Grow with a border-length-greedy BFS up to each window, then make local boundary moves
        that keep every piece connected and inside its window.
      - Make about 20 seeded attempts, reusing `plancheck.grow` where it fits.
      - The gate decides every candidate. The first that passes is `ok`; keep improving by
        border_km until the time limit, and keep the best candidate the gate has passed.
   2. **Per-piece `plancheck.drawable`** on {σ} ∪ attach. A piece proved undrawable alone gives a
      valid `support_ban` (MODEL §4.9, Proposition B, already [proved]).
   3. **Joint MILP, for proofs or as a fallback:**
      - x[z,i] with Σ_i x[z,i] = 1, plus the windows;
      - **one body per connected component of each piece's attach ZCTAs**: flow is rooted at the
        heaviest body and must reach every other body;
      - conditional wide-passage rows exactly as in `plancheck.wide_rows` (land-share conditions,
        `border_cm` rounding, connector rules);
      - lazy NeckCut rows;
      - a guiding objective, total cut border; never a zero objective (trap 19).
2. **docs/MODEL.md §4.10** proves one direction: every carve that passes M1 inside the windows
   satisfies every row. A HiGHS infeasibility proof is then a valid `holder_nogood`. The other
   direction is the gate's job. Necks inside attach states are in scope, because the gate checks
   the whole union. Until §4.10 is tagged [proved], a MILP infeasibility is reported as `unknown`
   with stop `milp_infeasible_claimed`.
3. **Measure in two steps.**
   1. NY in 4 pieces (shares from an existing plan) and one hard attach case, CT with RI.
   2. Only then CA in 4, TX in 3, PA in 3 and FL in 3.

   Record status, `found_by`, seconds to the first ok, total seconds and border_km.

Lane: `worker-advised` on `claude-bridge/claude-opus-5-5:high`. The Sol advisor uses Anthropic's
advisor timing. Sol reviews and verifies §4.10. Each lane is capped at 6 solver processes.
