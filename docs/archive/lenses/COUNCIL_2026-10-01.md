## The question

How should td make each planning channel's districts as close to their target τ_c as possible,
under the 51's rules: what to show stakeholders on 2026-10-02, and what the model should become.

## Situation

All four seats: **frame**. P_c (BALANCE §2.3) names no loss, and "district" means two objects: the
realizer's region (every ZIP of a unit, zero-opportunity ZIPs included) and the audit's ledger set
(ZIPs with a cell in the channel). The only precise statement, M_c(δ), answers a different
question: compactness inside a band.

## What the council established

Graded by the seats' evidence; "4/4" means every seat accepted it after cross-examination.

1. **The WH island is a floor for every connected map under this scenario (4/4 after C1; T).**
   WH's domain excludes the 8 WIFI states, the ZIP graph is built per state with rook adjacency,
   and AZ, CA, NV, OR, UT and WA reach the east only through ID, WY, CO and NM. So no WH district
   crosses, and 1.615 districts of mass west of the block forces, at K_WH = 11, a west district at
   or below −19.25% (two west districts summing to −0.385). No master row is used, so this binds
   ZIP-first maps (A8) too, and it survives reading a district as a region (BU13): WIFI states'
   ZIPs are outside WH's domain, not zero-opportunity ZIPs inside it. Only K, the domains (WIFI
   region, A6/A7) or non-contiguous ownership move it. Sol and Astra: at least one west district is
   that light, not necessarily both. 1.615 is #73's figure; no seat recomputed it.
2. **Drift is conserved inside exchange components (4/4 after C5; T).** (T2) gives the districts
   sharing a split unit exactly its mass, rounding gives a split ZIP only to districts that took
   flow from it, and zero-mass placement moves no mass. So before repair, drawn-minus-planned
   sums to zero over every set of districts linked through shared split units. Checked: NY
   (national_07/13, ±94.13), IL (WH_08/09, ±95.04), CA's four, MO (FI_02/13, ∓7.47). Repair is the
   one leak: FI's GA component lost 101.65 and FI_17, outside it, gained 101.65. Consequence:
   rebalancing moves districts toward their component's mean, and the plan fixes that mean. Not
   a rival to μ_S (Sol, Astra, Fable): μ_S stays the only pre-solve guarantee; conservation bounds
   sums, not single districts.
3. **Worst-district balance goes blind under a fixed outlier (4/4).** The three fixed WIFI blocks
   draw 782.9, 162.6 and 303.7 against τ 416.4, so the global worst deviation is about +88%
   whatever else changes, and in WH the island fixes −19.25%. A minimax loss then cannot rank
   improvements anywhere else. The seats proposed a sorted deviation vector (leximin) or
   per-component judging; the loss is the owner's (BU1).
4. **Margin and bounds are different instruments (4/4).** μ_S protects one rounding procedure; it
   is a restriction and bounds nothing (MODEL §4.7). The master with μ = 0 bounds only maps with
   the same supports and rules (Proposition D), and where a channel has free units its δ* comes
   from bisection: report the bracket, and treat its upper end as feasible, not as a bound
   (Astra, C4). The bound that holds whatever the rules is the island floor of item 1, and only
   for WH. Report the gap between bound and audited drawing; no certificate covers drawn balance.
5. **Plans sit on the band's edge (Fable, Opus; uncontested).** 6 of 13 national and 8 of 24 FI
   planned masses equal 0.9τ or 1.1τ to four figures in the margin-off run, so drift adds to 10%
   instead of to 0 (worst national +18.8% = edge + 8.8%). The objective, not only the margin,
   loses balance.
6. **855 of 872 audited pieces are display artifacts (Fable verified in pass 2; Sol asked for the
   test).** `output.py` (lines 32–36, 371–395) gives "connector ZIP not in ledger" to a ledger
   piece inside the realizer's main component, joined only through ZIPs with no cell in the
   channel. 17 pieces are real (13 corridor in WH_05, 4 shape).
7. **An L1 balance objective is linear and safe (4/4 on the math, after C3; T, short).** Copies on
   a support are clones, so Σ_j |a_j − τ| = Σ_S |Σ_v M_v t_{v,S} − τ n_S|: one slack per
   support. Claims 1–3 hold (they are about feasible points); the objective equality in Claim 1
   and Proposition D weakens to an inequality, which is still a lower bound. Not for tomorrow
   (all four): with μ = 0, drawing drift is larger than plan error at δ*.

## Recommendation

**For the 2026-10-02 maps** (three seats; Sol keeps (a) as the baseline it would show if (b) does not
audit better):

1. Margin off, which needs a small flag: `supports.margin()` has no switch, and #73 zeroed it in
   memory (Opus, Fable).
2. Split WH and FI at the WIFI block into their domain components, at the current K allocation
   (WH 2 west + 9 east, FI 3 + 21); leave national joint, since `spec.check_national` requires one
   national channel and its parts already fit (4.027 + 8.973). The split loses no support (no
   connected support crosses the cut) and frees the east from the island's δ: in the joint
   bisection one δ is set by the worst component, which lets compactness park eastern districts at
   ±19%. It is not the joint solve: each part plans around its own mean (west WH 0.8075τ, east
   1.0428τ), so report deviations against the parent τ_c, which needs a small script over
   `districts.csv` (Sol and Astra rejected "exact, no new code" as first stated; Opus and Fable
   conceded it).
3. Plan each part at its own bisected δ* (+ tolerance), compactness second, with existing code.
4. Optionally, a time-boxed swap pass inside split states toward τ, accepted only when the worse
   district of the pair improves and contiguity holds; the existing audit judges it.
5. Report every drawn deviation against ±10% with the audit's fail verdict, not a loosened band;
   δ*(μ=0) labelled as a bound for maps under these supports and rules only; the island floor for
   WH; WIFI's ±88% as set by policy; the legacy map's 31/51 as comparator; 17 real pieces with the
   855 artifacts explained.
6. Keep the tiny cell (ZIP 13027 FI, 1.6e-8 m_rel): exclude cells below LP tolerance from the
   transport, place them by adjacency, keep their true mass in the ledger, and list them. Not a
   balance blocker (2.4e-11 τ), but the diagnostic's zeroing left the cell unowned with a wrong
   reason, and the one-owner audit still passed on 11,720 of 11,721 cells (Opus, Fable). If not
   fixed in time, disclose it.
7. A K_WH = 13 or moved-WIFI variant only if the owner allows it, labelled as a business change
   (island 1.909 → −4.5%).

**For the durable model:** balance is the objective (the owner's loss; L1 is linear, minimax is
the existing bisection, leximin over exchange components was proposed), compactness a tie-break,
the band an audit device. One definition of a district, the region including zero-opportunity
ZIPs, shared by realizer and audit unless BU13 says otherwise. The loader reports per-component
mass/τ and the integer residue before solving, so floors like the island are known before any
solve. Keep the support master with μ = 0 as the bound and seed; the fine level becomes a
rebalance inside exchange components (A3 per component), or heavy ZIPs enter the master as binary
atoms inside t_{v,S} so the family does not grow (Fable). Repair may not move mass across
components unless the worst deviation falls.

  shrunk band's edge, against the owner's priority.

## Confidence and what would change it

Medium, all four. High for the island floor, drift conservation and the edge-planning reading:
they rest on code paths and run files. Untested: that the split, flag and swap pass build and
audit in one day, and the size of the gap. Would change: national or FI still above ±5% after a
deviation-minimising drawing (then the binding rows come first); the owner letting districts
cross zero-opportunity or WIFI ground (dissolves the island); a count-within-±x% loss (changes
what to optimise); exchange failing to bring NY or IL within about 3% (heavy ZIPs must enter the
plan).
