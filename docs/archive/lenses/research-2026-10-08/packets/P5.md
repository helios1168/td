# P5: Carving one unit into k connected in-band pieces with prescribed terminals (supporting)
Question: What is known about partitioning a vertex-weighted planar graph (one state's ZIP graph,
~100-1,700 vertices) into exactly k connected pieces with each piece's weight in a window
[lo_i, hi_i], where some pieces must contain a prescribed set of boundary vertices? Cover:
complexity of balanced connected k-partition (BCP) on general, planar and grid graphs;
approximation results; exact MILP, branch-and-cut and compact formulations with reported scale;
polynomial cases (trees, series-parallel, k = 2); heuristics with guarantees; and the
"prescribed terminals" or "rooted" variants (each piece contains a given root). Known names:
Chlebíková 1996 (BCP2 approximation), Lucertini-Perl-Simeone 1993, Ito-Zhou-Nishizeki (weight
bounds), Chataigner-Salgado-Wakabayashi 2007, Miyazawa-Moura-Ota-Wakabayashi 2021 (cuts for
BCP), Zhou-Wang et al. 2019+, "connected k-partition" surveys, and Carter et al. 2020 App. C.1
(kb carter2020: draw whole districts so the remainder stays connected and touches the border).
Deliver: the complexity line for our exact parameters, the fastest exact method reported at our
scale, and whether any formulation handles windows plus rooted pieces together.
