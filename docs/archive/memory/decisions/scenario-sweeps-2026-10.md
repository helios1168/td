# Choices in the 2026-10-01/02 sweeps, council and stakeholder maps

**Status:** recorded 2026-10-02 from td orchestrator session 01a0f177 on m5; working choices for diagnostics and the stakeholder deliverable, not changes to td's code or scenarios. Policy choices (distance cap, WIFI, margin, K_WH) stay with the owner. Measurements: `facts/scenario-sweeps-2026-10`.

**Decision.**
- **Diagnostic workarounds stay local:** FI ZIP 13027's cell is zeroed and the ZCTA polygon read is batched only in local wrappers and stage-2 runs; fixing them in td needs a review round (triage item I3). WH ran at δ 0.20 in the no-margin run because 0.10 is proved infeasible there.
- **Compute:** lever sweeps fan out one process per (setting, channel, margin) at `nice -n 10` with `xargs -P16` on m5, after the owner asked for full use of the machine and then noticed 100% CPU. A small resumable Python dispatcher (`runs/sweep/dispatch.py`, m5 16 slots, m2 11) was written instead of installing GNU parallel on both Studios (Brewfile change); m2 jobs use a separate clone `/Users/Shared/sv-ntlee/td-sweep` reusing m2's venv by symlink, with the extract copied over the LAN and checked by sha256.
- **Dollar conversion** at each channel's own factor, so channels sum to the owner's totals and the wells_fi gap shows. "Western states override" was read as the 8-state WIFI block. WIFI states' national cells count as WIFI, as in the 51; IFA is planned separately (owner: no interaction with the other channels, but its maps stay in the grid), with free states by the owner's M/(2K) rule at each K.
- **Grid shape:** about ±20% around each target K (national 12–16, WH 9–13, FI 18–26, IFA 44–56), WIFI as the leftover 1 or 2 districts; the WIFI axis trimmed to 4 regions to stay near 600 solves; "7 low" became "6 low" (no NM); a 1,600 km mountain/plains cap added as a variant, left to the owner as policy; free states are the 51's list plus any state over half a district; stage 2 plans at each part's smallest band + 0.005 (at least ±2%), judged at ±10%, per the council, and runs each channel's best and runner-up configurations. The national purity check is dropped in "fall back" runs. Automatic stage-2 picks were replaced by hand picks M1 (forced splits only) and M2 (hybrid) after the picker chose ±30%. Bands were extended to ±25% and ±30% for WH; split caps are 2 per split state or the state's size rounded up (+1 loose). The combined New England channel stays whole-state only; WH was rerun at 12 with each middle block. Quick split variants stopped on 2026-10-02 because more runs couldn't beat the drift without a model change before the deadline. The thin-link threshold is 25 km of shared border between a district's pieces in different states.
- **Council (2026-10-01):** four seats, no solo lens, one pass of independent reports merging council fields with Gromov lens fields, then one cross-examination (owner, for speed); six claims relayed to all four seats in one shared packet; cited issues and archive files exported to gitignored `runs/council_2026-10-01/evidence/` because advisors can't read GitHub or tags. The memo was committed to public `main` before triage approval (it holds only descaled opportunity and model output); the triage itself stays unapplied pending the owner; "what limits K_WH or the WIFI region" goes to #75 as a comment.
- **BALANCE.md brief:** written in MODEL.md notation as Unicode math; P_c states the owner's problem formally with BU1, BU13 and BU2 open; §3 renamed "Where balance is lost" with a pointer to §2.4–2.6; layout papers left unnamed (no `references.bib` keys); only the Gromov item in §9 rewritten; the commit was pushed to `main`; STATE.md rewritten to 560 bytes, which fixed the size-cap test that had failed since 0720f49 (1,050 bytes against 1,024).
- **Stakeholder maps (2026-10-02, d72df91 and db58fd3):** options A, B and C, today's 51 and IFA at K 49, each the best drawn map per policy choice; display fill from state-clipped ZIP Voronoi cells instead of ZCTA polygons (holes and slivers); the legacy `plan_summary` look kept, without the old `--groups` outlines or a comparison cover page; the deck built from page images (no pypdf in the venv); render scripts left out of the repo. #81 benchmarks against the 18-split map, not the 51 or the 13/12/23 cell.
- **NE + okks follow-up (later on 2026-10-02, same session):** the strict may-split list is states above 1.1 districts, the arithmetic minimum at ±10%, not "above 1 district", because TN and TX in FI (0.97 each) fit inside one district. For the national-only redraw, WH's and FI's longer may-split lists and earlier plans were kept, so national is the only channel that changes; with identical settings the solves are deterministic, so their splits should match the 14-split map. The redraw used stage 1's clean national plan (CA FL NY TX, ±9.4%) and WH plan (4 splits, ±8%) instead of the picker's choices, which corrects the picker, not the approach. East-shape ranking considers only FI plans within ±8.5%, about 1.5 points inside ±10%, to leave room for drawing drift. Only the Philadelphia metro was carved: Washington and NYC carves leave disconnected MD and NJ remnants, which `td/spec.py` rejects for whole units, and fixing that needs remnant carves.
- **Map shortlist (2026-10-02):** it lives in gitignored `runs/shortlist/` rather than tracked `docs/figures/`, because td tracks only the figures `docs/RESULTS.md` cites and most shortlisted maps are working drafts. Tiers: presentable (passes, every district within ±10%, looks acceptable), contender (passes or nearly, with a known defect) and reference (fails or is superseded but instructive). IFA sits in tier 1 as a separate channel.
- **Council triage close-out (2026-10-02):** applying the triage waited for the owner's Annotate review, since filing issues and pushing to public `main` are hard to undo. In fork 01a0febf the findings were grouped by theme for reading, while the memo kept the flat numbered table, so each finding stays in exactly one bin, as the triage rule requires.
