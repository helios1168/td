# Lessons
* [A channel-filtered probe solves an easier problem: slicing the data can drop states silently, and feasibility conclusions do not transfer](lessons/filtered-probe-easier-problem.md) - ACCEPTED: A feasibility probe filtered to the national channel silently dropped ND/SD, solved an easier instance, and reported 1,200 km district caps sufficient; the full data needs 1,500 km. Feasibility probes over a channel slice must be re-run unfiltered before committing compute.
* [Map labels must come from the gate's whole-map verdict on unrounded full-extract dollars; every derived scorer drifts](lessons/labels-from-the-gate-unrounded.md) - ACCEPTED: A CONUS-only merged extract read FI about 2% high; IFA is stored in relative units (x1.252) and `score.py` had no per-district IFA numbers; the gate compared rounded; the IFA index matched names, not verdicts. The fixes landed and an independent re-gate confirmed all 12 labelled maps.
* [A solver segfault is a crash, not evidence: contain SCIP's time-limit faults and retry once with heuristics off](lessons/solver-crash-is-unknown-not-infeasible.md) - ACCEPTED: SCIP faults at its own time limit (six P3 crashes on one nw49-48 window). The landed fix records the crash, retries once with heuristics off (the same window solved to optimal in 261 s), files unknown rather than infeasible or connected, and never grows a window from a crash.
