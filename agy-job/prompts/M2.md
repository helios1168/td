## Objective

Add an opt-in `--stage2-rematch` flag to the `plan_realise.py` CLI, defaulting off. The disabled
path must preserve current outputs and behavior. When enabled, run the terminal rematch only after
all bundles have completed realization, overlap handoff, repair, and optional `--sweep-zips`, and
before writing the final CSV/JSON outputs.

The final realized cell map is `cell_of`: `(zip, fine_channel) -> (district, bundle, wholesaler)`.
The fixed geometry is the district label of every realized cell. Rematching may change only the
representative attached to a district. It must not change `cell_of` district labels, district mass,
ZIP membership, pieces, contiguity, bands, overlap/handoff records, or sweep results.

## Exact realized valuation

Construct the terminal valuation from the final realized cells, not from state shares or the
pre-handoff labels. For each realized district, aggregate its own assigned fine channels over its
assigned ZIP cells into a synthetic graph suitable for `td.channel.gain_matrix`:

- one synthetic node per `(zip, district)` that has at least one cell in that district;
- `M` is the sum of that node's assigned `M_c` values;
- `S` is rep-major and sums assigned `S_c` values by representative;
- `S_free` sums assigned `S_free_c` values;
- use the global representatives in the original staffing order, and pass them explicitly so
  every representative prices every district;
- preserve deterministic district order and do not modify the source graph or `cell_of`.

The district bundle/channel membership comes from `district_rows` or the cell ownership itself.
Do not include cells from another district at the same ZIP merely because the ZIP is shared by
bundles. This is the discrete counterpart of
`g[i,j] = sum_{z in A_j} sum_{c in B_j} u_i(z,c)`.
