### 0.5 M1, exactly as the gate computes it

`td.audit.check_m1` (called by `tools/mandates/check.py::m1` through `score.m1_check`) is the gate; PROBLEM.md names it as the check (PROBLEM.md:115 `the polygon contiguity check `td/audit.py::check_m1` (#108), run by every td run`). Let X ⊆ V be one district's ZCTA set in channel c, Z⁰_c the ZCTAs all of whose rows in c have m = 0 (td/audit.py:1016 `zero = {z for z, m in chm[ch].items() if not m > 0}     # R3: no opportunity in ch`). The district passes iff all three hold:

**(P) One piece.** G_adj[X] is connected, where G_adj = (V, E_poly ∪ E_conn) (td/audit.py:941 `adj = adjacency(run.polygon, gaps=gap_width)    # R5: land-only gap edges join pieces too`; td/audit.py:1021 `if len(comps) < 2:`). A district with ≥ 2 components has detached pieces and the map fails; "listing" is not enough (PROBLEM.md:66 `included. No tolerance: one detached piece fails the map, and listing it is not enough. The`).

**(N) No neck.** Write a(·) for land area (`aland`). A *neck* is a set A ⊆ X, connected on E_poly ∪ E_conn ∪ (land-only gap edges), with
  0.05 · a(X) ≤ a(A) ≤ a(X ∖ A) (the cut-off part is the *smaller side*; the rest may lie in pieces), and
  w(A) < 10 km,
where w(A) is the minimum, over placements of relays on either side, of the total width of the edges leaving A in the *width graph* H_X (td/audit.py:461 `NECK_W_KM = 10.0        # owner, 2026-10-05 (#121): a passage narrower than this is a neck; never tuned`, td/audit.py:462 `NECK_SHARE = 0.05       # owner: a part holding this share of the district's land area`). H_X has vertex set X ∪ R(X). Edge widths, in whole centimetres, each floored (`border_cm`, td/audit.py:485 `def border_cm(metres: float) -> int:`; a neck has width ≤ NECK_W_CM − 1, td/audit.py:466 `NECK_W_CM = round(NECK_W_KM * 1e5)`):

  - approved connector: ∞ (R1; the old rule "0 where land would do" survives only as an opt-in, td/audit.py:938 `land_rule = os.environ.get(LAND_RULE_ENV) == "1"`);
  - land in no ZCTA between two ZCTAs of one state (R2, `gap_width`): the shared Voronoi border of the `dropped` rows, because the land-only file is absent (td/audit.py:562 `and b not in self.connector.get(a, ()) and self.state.get(a) == self.state.get(b)):`);

### 0.7 Structure worth stating before the approaches (all (inferred) from §0.2–0.5)

Given the channel domains, the K_c and the windows, the main-map problem separates by channel: channel c asks for a partition of its footprint's ZCTAs into K_c blocks, each (i) M1-local-feasible and (ii) of mass in [L_c, U_c]; the coverage rule (C) is exactly the partition requirement within each channel. Channels interact only through (a) the domain partition (which cells belong to which channel), (b) the total K ∈ [48, 54], and (c) the sum in the split-state count. Every approach below plans or draws per channel. **Correction (review §3.6):** separation holds as mathematics for feasibility: with domains and K fixed, distinct planning channels partition different fine-channel cells, their district partitions may occupy the same physical ZCTA without conflict, and there is no shared resource constraint. It does not carry over to a map-level shape or balance *objective* unless that objective is additive or otherwise separable. What forces equal-or-disjoint state sets in some tools is an implementation limit of the assembly path, not a theorem about national feasibility: `merge.py` requires ZIP-disjoint sources (tools/exp/contig/merge.py:86 `if z in seen:`; §14, §18).

A rootless selected component contradicts the sum of its flow inequalities; a spanning-tree flow satisfies them for every connected district. Actual P-adjacency, not relay paths; the shipped R5 land-gap adjacency is empty, and any later committed R5 data must enter this graph consistently.

**Hard N-connectivity.** For a candidate X build the width graph H_hc(X) from all of X, the static zero-opportunity relays reachable from X, the current polygon, connector and gap widths, and no area contribution from relays outside X. For every nonempty proper A ⊂ X connected on the district's **own** P-graph with 0.05 a(X) ≤ a(A) ≤ 0.5 a(X): min over relay sets T of cap_H(δ(A ∪ T)) ≥ W = 10 km (1,000,000 cm in the gate's integer widths), except exactly the authorized Manhattan violation. **Splits and objective.** R_hcs = Σ_j b_hcjs; 2 p_hcs ≤ R_hcs ≤ q_h + 53 p_hcs (p = 1 exactly when at least two districts own territory in that channel-state). Objective: lexmin (Φ_shape, Φ_balance, Σ p_hcs) subject to all hard constraints. Contiguity and the dollar window are feasibility requirements, not penalties; shape has no hard gate; K moves inside the permitted total range.

Each added row cuts off the current solution (all of Sep is unowned by j at that solution), so the loop terminates (inferred), but the number of rounds is not bounded polynomially. **Review C10 (verified, with scope):** a path from a selected vertex of the detached component to the retained root (or to the selected b in the rootless case) crosses each stated separator; for the second ring take the path's first entry into the old main component, whose preceding vertex is both near-`main` and reachable without entering `main`. **Correction (review §3.2): restricted-first.** `_attempt` runs the DAG restriction first for every group and band; the complete model runs second and, with `prove` false, only when the restriction found no connected drawing (tools/exp/contig/draw.py:410 `if not dag and not prove and out is not None and out.status == "connected":`). ### 4.2 `--sequential`

One split unit (or adjacent split units entered through one another) at a time; each district's part is attached to the *union* of what it already owns next to the unit, taken as one body; units not yet drawn count at their *planned* masses in the band row (`extra`). ### 4.5 Soundness questions at this layer

  1. **Arm-1 restriction.** Whole units and the plan's holder lists are fixed. "Infeasible" proves nothing about the map, only about the group under the plan's support (draw docstring; docs/RESULTS.md:557-559). The CT + NJ via NY district is the recurring example: drawn infeasible/unknown at every δ, repaired only with ≈1,000-ZCTA windows (docs/RESULTS.md:585-588).
  2. **Objective ordering is not strictly lexicographic.** The border term is scaled to ≤ 900, the land term to ≤ 900 per group, the moment term ≤ 9, each "below one split" (1,000), but they add: the total shape/land cost can reach ≈ 1,809 > 1,000 (inferred from draw.py:82, 85 and the term construction), so a solution with one more holder can be preferred. Review C13 (verified): the range of the variation, 900 + 900 + 9 = 1809, exceeds the 1000 charge for an additional holder, so the scalar objective does not encode strict holder-first lexicography. The border term carries a constant offset through negative own-body credits (tools/exp/contig/draw.py:866 `cost[col[z, k]] -= b_scale * x`), so it is the term's **range**, not its raw signed value, that the argument uses.
  3. **Sequential mode** clamps the band row for districts already past the band, so a sequential drawing can leave districts out of the window; only the later repair/gate catches it (tools/exp/contig/draw.py:954; docs/RESULTS.md:638-642 `**Review fixes** (Sol review 35e038f8). The window's band row had borrowed the`).
  4. **`prune`** discards (z, j) pairs whose cheapest root-body path already exceeds hi − F_j; valid when j has a root body (path mass counts only free ZCTAs, bodies at 0) (tools/exp/contig/draw.py:547). I checked the logic, not a proof. Review C12 (verified for its stated fixed-body model): if a feasible connected district owns z it contains a root–z path, and with non-negative masses and body masses zeroed on the contracted path the cheapest path costs no more than the district's free mass, at most hi − F_j; without a root, a connected selected set lies in one allowed component, so a component of insufficient total mass leaves no subset that meets the lower bound. This does not validate the fixed supports, omitted bodies or the sequential decomposition themselves.
  5. **NeckCut validity** is argued in the docstring and "checked by enumeration on a toy" (docs/RESULTS.md:716 `checked by enumeration on a toy), so "optimal" is an optimum of the cut-augmented model and`); I did not re-derive it. **Correction (review C14):** the validity statement holds only under the old connected-anchor hypotheses, and the model it is checked against is stricter than current M1 (no relays, width-only edges missing, old connector widths: §4.3 correction), so "optimal" is an optimum of a cut-augmented model that can exclude drawings the gate accepts.
  6. **Group decomposition is exact in the joint mode** (inferred): groups share no free ZCTA and no district, and F_j is constant, so the band rows and the objective separate; `--sequential` is not exact (4.2). Review C25 (verified, scope): the grouping joins free components whenever they share an allowed district (tools/exp/contig/draw.py:349 `parent[find(qi)] = find(first[j])`), so no free variable or participating district crosses final groups and fixed-body masses are constants: P and mass feasibility factor, and optimality factors for the objective actually assembled as a sum of group objectives. It does not establish equivalence to an independently specified map-wide objective (normalisation and any cross-group unit or land terms must match), and it does not cover sequential mode or a new shape objective.
  7. **Tolerances are in the extract's units.** On the dollar extract (masses in $M) the draw accepts a given or constructed start with a band slack `tol = 1e-7·max(1, hi)` 1e-7 × 1,035 $M = 0.0001035 $M = $103.50 at the FI top (tools/exp/contig/draw.py:1119 `tol = 1e-7 * max(1.0, hi)`), and the master certifies planned masses only to `FEAS_TOL` = 1e-6 of τ (≈ $1,000–1,400 on τ ≈ $1,000–1,400M, td/master.py:66). The final whole-dollar gate (tools/looks/score.py:337) is the arbiter, so these slacks matter only for edge cases (inferred). Review C26 (verified): the start validator applies that tolerance to its stored rows, so it can accept a band-row excess of that size; this numerical acceptance is not the whole-dollar gate's acceptance and is not a new permissible balance tolerance.
  8. **Connector widths**: draw/repair/plancheck/master-contact still use the pre-2026-10-14 connector rule while the gate uses R1 (docs/MODEL.md:884-889), so the rows can be stricter than the gate on connectors (follow-up the repo already lists). **Correction (review §3.1):** the connector widths are one of three mismatches; relays and width-only edges are the other two (§4.3 correction, C14), and "fix the connector rule" alone does not make the rows match the gate.
