# Economic-theory citations for matching and stability work

The citations that did real work for U2-stab (2026-09-02).

From `~/resources/economic-theory/FOUNDATIONS.md` (cite in bold):
- **Roth 1984**: stability, not efficiency, decides survival; the argument to leadership.
- **Roth & Sotomayor 1990**: the catch-all reference.
- **Gale & Shapley 1962**: background only here.
- **Roth 1982** is not usable for "does uniqueness weaken the strategy-proofness obstruction";
  nothing in FOUNDATIONS settles that question.
- **Caragiannis et al. 2019** applies to the EF1 verdict only if the unselected reps are
  released (★1).

From `docs/foundations/LIT_economic-theory.bib` (cite in code font, as pointers):
- `eeckhout2000`: SPC implies a unique stable matching equal to greedy top-pair. Stated for
  balanced one-to-one markets; the 111-against-13 unbalanced version with an outside option is
  proved directly in `docs/units/U2-stab.md` `## Model` §3.
- `consuegra2013`: SPC is sufficient only; never write "unique iff".
- `clark2006`: the No Crossing Condition, the fallback only if alignment is broken (for example
  by a rep-specific travel-cost term).
- `niederle2009`: with aligned preferences, parties renegotiate an unstable announcement
  themselves.
- `aignerhorev2022`: envy-free matching across a selection boundary.
- `gan2019`: m < n house allocation, unit demand only.
- `echenique2024`: the EC record is a conference abstract; the full text is arXiv:2402.13378 and
  was not fetched. Cite for framing only unless the arXiv text is pulled.

Withdrawn: `fotakis2014` (in `literature/RESEARCH_ADDITIONS.bib`) and Gibbard-Satterthwaite as
a basis for ★8 (AGENTS.md trap 17).
